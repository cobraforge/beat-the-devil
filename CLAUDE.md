# Beat the Devil

Before any work on this project, read `DESIGN.md`. It holds the design rules
(five bolts, the 2.0 s penalty = invulnerability, flame height vs. the player
ceiling, the aim/lock/fire telegraph contract, survive-phase hazard limits,
curves-only claws) and the reasons behind each. A change that breaks one of
them is a regression. Keep the numbers in `DESIGN.md` in sync with the code.

`README.md` documents how the systems are built; `DESIGN.md` documents why.
